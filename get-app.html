Get the CropDoctor app

Take a photo of a crop leaf and get the disease and treatment in Kannada and English. The app needs an internet connection to analyze photos.

ಆ್ಯಪ್ ಡೌನ್‌ಲೋಡ್ ಮಾಡಿDownload for Android (APK)

  1. ಮೇಲಿನ ಡೌನ್‌ಲೋಡ್ ಬಟನ್ ಒತ್ತಿ.

    Tap the download button above.

  2. ಡೌನ್‌ಲೋಡ್ ಆದ ಫೈಲ್ ತೆರೆಯಿರಿ. ಫೋನ್ ಕೇಳಿದರೆ, ನಿಮ್ಮ ಬ್ರೌಸರ್‌ಗೆ "Install unknown apps" ಅನುಮತಿ ನೀಡಿ.

    Open the downloaded file. If your phone asks, allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser.

  3. "Install" ಒತ್ತಿ, ನಂತರ CropDoctor ತೆರೆಯಿರಿ.

    Tap Install, then open CropDoctor.

Your phone shows a warning because the app is not from the Play Store. This is normal for any app installed from a link. If Play Protect asks, choose Install anyway.

iPhone cannot install APK files. Open this site in Safari, tap the Share button, then tap Add to Home Screen. It will work like an app.

Prefer not to install? Use CropDoctor in your browser.
