04 / About

How the service works

CropDoctor sends a submitted leaf image to an analysis endpoint and displays the response it receives. The result is an automated estimate, not a confirmed diagnosis.

Your photo is processed by the Netlify analysis function and Google’s Gemini API to produce the result; see the privacy notice. Do not rely on an assessment alone to choose a treatment.
