04 — About

Under the hood

CropDoctor uses a vision AI model that recognises any vegetable or crop from a leaf photo — no crop selection needed — and detects healthy or diseased conditions, so coverage is not limited to a fixed list.

Each prediction is paired with a curated disease database covering cause, severity, and recommended treatment, so the output is immediately actionable rather than just a label.
