Privacy notice

Last updated: 18 November 2026

CropDoctor is a student project of the BCA department at SA College, Naregal, Karnataka, India. This notice explains in plain language what happens to the photo you upload.

What we process

  • Your leaf photo. When you click “Analyze Leaf”, your browser shrinks the photo, re-encodes it as a JPEG (which normally removes embedded details such as GPS location) and sends it to our analysis function on Netlify.
  • The result. The function passes the photo to Google’s Gemini API, which returns the crop, condition and advice that appear on your screen.
  • Technical data. Netlify and Google may record technical information such as your IP address and request time, as part of running their services.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask you to create an account and we do not ask for your name, phone number or location.
  • We do not intentionally save your photos or results on our own server or database.
  • We do not use advertising or tracking cookies and we do not sell personal data.

Third parties

  • Netlify hosts the website and runs the analysis function.
  • Google (Gemini API) analyses the photo. Depending on the Gemini API plan in use, Google’s terms may allow it to keep submitted content and use it to improve its products. Read Google’s current terms for details.
  • Google Fonts serves the page fonts, so your browser contacts Google when the page loads.

Because of this, please upload only photos of plants. Do not upload photos that show people, faces, documents, vehicle number plates or private places.

Your choices

You decide whether to upload a photo. If you do not want a photo processed, do not click “Analyze Leaf”. Because we do not keep photos or results, we cannot look up or delete them later; to ask about data held by Netlify or Google, contact those companies.
