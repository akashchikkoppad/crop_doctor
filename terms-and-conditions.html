1. What the service does

You upload a photo of a plant, and an AI model gives an automated estimate of the crop and its condition, with suggested next steps in Kannada and English.

2. Not professional advice

  • Results are estimates and can be wrong. The AI may misidentify a crop, miss a disease or suggest the wrong cause.
  • The service is not a substitute for a qualified agriculture officer, agronomist or the local Raita Samparka Kendra. Please confirm a result with them before treating a crop.
  • If a result suggests a pesticide or other chemical, read the product label, follow the dose and safety instructions, and take local expert advice first. We are not responsible for crop loss or harm caused by relying on a result.

3. Your photos
